Our Mission

Rafiq Foundation is dedicated to creating positive change in communities through sustainable development, education, healthcare, and social welfare initiatives. We believe in the power of collective action to transform lives and build a more equitable society.

Our approach combines grassroots engagement with strategic partnerships to deliver impactful programs that address the root causes of social challenges. We work tirelessly to empower individuals and communities to reach their full potential.

Our Vision

Founded in 1992 by Haji Rafiq Awan (Late), Rafiq Foundation began with a school in Muzaffargarh and has since expanded to build schools, mosques, and education complexes across Pakistan.

For over 25 years, we've served humanity through clean water projects, orphan sponsorships, food distribution, and disaster relief, empowering communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development.
